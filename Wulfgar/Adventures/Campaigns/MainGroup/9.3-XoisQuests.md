## Dead City of Xois
The city of Xois, once the "food capital" of the Nhar city-states, has become a necropolis under the control of **VAMPIRE_NECROMANCER**.

She is a general of Carthus responsible for raising and controling the armies of corpses left from the attacks of **Irrah**. She has turned almost all of the population of Xois into zombies, ghools, skeletons or vampire thralls under her command.
