# Xois Quest
___
After defeating Irrah, the party is given an ancient magical ship SHIP_NAME by the obsidian sphinx to aid their journey to Carthus.

But before they are able to reach the ancient city of Carthus they must pass through the now dead city of Xois.


## The Journey
The party journeys down the Nhar river for nine days in the direction of Xois. As they pass abandoned and or devastated villages they may chose to stop and investigate or carry on forward.

### Abandoned Villages
Stopping on any of the many abandoned and or devastated villages the party can investigate them and reval the following relevant information.

- *(DC 10)* The villages closest to Shidai seem to have been abandoned but those near Xois were raided.

- *(DC 15)* The raiders didn't take any "treasure" but they have always destroyed temples of the Holy Path.

- *(DC 20)* The tracks that remain from the closest villages to Xois indicate that the villagers were dragged in the direction of the city.


### Rogue Fisherman
After six days of travel the party crosses paths with a strange boat in the river. Its a fishing boat with six oars at the ready flying the blue and yellow of Xois.

As they begin to approach the fishing boat they start to signal something, a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check from a character with proficiency in water vehicles will give them the following message:

*"Do not approach, do not approach enemy armada close"*
___
After which the boat begins to row towards the shore as five of tentacles made of spines rise from the depths of the water. **Roll initiative!**


> ##### DM Note
> The tentacles want to destroy the fishing boat but not kill the fisherman or the player characters.
>
> If they lose half or more of their hit points they will escape the fight at the end of their next turn.

## Dead City of Xois
The city of Xois, once the "food capital" of the Nhar city-states, has become a necropolis shrouded in a seemingly eternal early night under the control of **Lilith the Grafter**.

She is a general of Carthus responsible for raising and controling the armies of corpses left from the attacks of **Irrah**. She has turned almost all of the population of Xois into zombies, ghools, skeletons or vampire thralls under her command.

___
***Lilith the Grafter.*** is not a single person but a cohort of peoples charmed by her and transformed into her hivemind via a vampiric brain worm.
